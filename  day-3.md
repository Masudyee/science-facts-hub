# Daily Science Insight - Day 3

### Title: Bananas Are Radioactively Charging You!

Did you know? The yellow, delicious bananas that we eat every day are actually slightly radioactive! But don't worry, it is completely harmless.

* **The Science:** Bananas are rich in Potassium. A very small natural fraction of that potassium is Potassium-40, which is a radioactive isotope. 
* **The Scale:** Because of this, every single banana emits a tiny amount of radiation. In fact, radiation experts even use a funny informal measurement unit called the "Banana Equivalent Dose" (BED) to compare radiation levels!
* **Should you worry?** Not at all. You would need to eat roughly 10,000,000 (10 million) bananas at once to get a fatal dose of radiation, which is humanly impossible.

So go ahead and enjoy your banana without any fear!
