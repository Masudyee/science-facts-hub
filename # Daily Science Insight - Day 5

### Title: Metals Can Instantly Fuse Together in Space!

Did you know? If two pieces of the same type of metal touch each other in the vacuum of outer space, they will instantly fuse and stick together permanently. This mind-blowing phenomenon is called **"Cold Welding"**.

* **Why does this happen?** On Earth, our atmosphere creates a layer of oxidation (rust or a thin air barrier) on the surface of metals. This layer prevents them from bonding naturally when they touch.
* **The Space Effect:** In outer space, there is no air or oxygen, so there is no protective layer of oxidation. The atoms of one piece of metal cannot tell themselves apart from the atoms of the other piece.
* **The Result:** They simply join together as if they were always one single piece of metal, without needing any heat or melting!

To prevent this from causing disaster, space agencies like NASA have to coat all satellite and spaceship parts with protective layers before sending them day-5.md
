# Daily Science Insight - Day 5

### Title: Metals Can Instantly Fuse Together in Space!

Did you know? If two pieces of the same type of metal touch each other in the vacuum of outer space, they will instantly fuse and stick together permanently. This mind-blowing phenomenon is called **"Cold Welding"**.

* **Why does this happen?** On Earth, our atmosphere creates a layer of oxidation (rust or a thin air barrier) on the surface of metals. This layer prevents them from bonding naturally when they touch.
* **The Space Effect:** In outer space, there is no air or oxygen, so there is no protective layer of oxidation. The atoms of one piece of metal cannot tell themselves apart from the atoms of the other piece.
* **The Result:** They simply join together as if they were always one single piece of metal, without needing any heat or melting!

To prevent this from causing disaster, space agencies like NASA have to coat all satellite and spaceship parts with protective layers before sending them into space.
